Quasiperiodic Function Visualizations

Interactive tools for exploring torus geodesics and level curves

Flat Torus Geodesics (2D)

Click in the square to choose a direction from the origin. The line wraps around the opposite edges, just like motion on a flat torus. Rational slopes close after a finite number of wraps, while irrational slopes keep spreading through the square.

Embedded Torus Geodesics (3D)

Click on the torus to choose a direction from the orange starting point. The animation shows how the path winds around the surface. Some paths close, while others keep covering more of the torus.

Level Set Explorer & Novikov's Problem

Explore level curves of f(x,y) = cos(2πx) + cos(2πy) + cos(2παx) in a movable window of the plane. Blue curves close inside the current view, while orange curves reach the edge and continue outside the visible window. Adjust α and the level value c to see how the pattern changes.
