Understanding the Result

Rational Slope (e.g., 1/2, 2/3):
The line closes after a finite number of wraps. This creates a repeating path.

Irrational Slope (e.g., √2, φ, π):
The line does not close. Over time, it passes close to every region of the square.

The Flat Torus:
The square with opposite edges identified. When the line hits an edge, it wraps to the opposite edge and continues.
